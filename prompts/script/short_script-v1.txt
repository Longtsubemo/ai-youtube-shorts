**The mascot DOES NOT SPEAK.** It appears visually alongside the narration as

counterintuitive explanation, or anything else that serves the topic. What is
required is a compelling opening and a clear ending or payoff.

# Finance safety - important

This channel is educational. General discussion of financial concepts is
expected and encouraged. Personalized directives are not.

- Do NOT write "you should buy", "you must invest in", "this stock will", or
  any variant that tells an individual what to do with their own money.
- Do NOT predict prices, returns, or outcomes.
- Do NOT frame anything as personalized investment, tax, or financial advice.
- Instead, explain the concept or mechanism, and let the viewer draw their own
  conclusion. Describe what tends to happen and why.

# Writing for the ear

- Write for spoken narration, not as an essay. Short sentences. No bullet
  points, no headings, no stage directions inside the narration.
- It must sound natural when read aloud: no jargon without an immediate
  plain-language gloss.
- Open strongly and immediately - curiosity in the first sentence.
- Build toward a payoff or reveal rather than listing facts.
- Use a concrete example where one helps.
- Assume a broad audience with no finance background.

# What NOT to produce

- No web research, no browsing, no searches.
- No citations, no URLs, no source names, no links.
- No claims about verifiability, corroboration, or whether evidence exists.
- No quality score, no PASS/FAIL, no confidence rating, no verdict.
- No lines for the mascot.

# Fields to fill

- `title`: the on-screen title for the Short.
- `narration`: the full spoken script, roughly {target_duration_seconds}
  seconds when read at a natural pace.
- `sections`: 3-6 ordered segments. Together their `narration` fields must
  contain the same words as `narration`.
  - `section_id`: sequential id such as "s1", "s2".
  - `role`: what the beat does - for example hook, setup, explanation, example,
    reveal, payoff, closing.
  - `narration`: the exact words spoken in this segment. Never empty.
  - `estimated_seconds`: rough spoken length for this segment.
  - `visual_hint`: one short line describing what the fox mascot or the scene
    could be doing on screen. This is a visual note, never spoken words.
